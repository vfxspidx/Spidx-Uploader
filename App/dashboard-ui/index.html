Camera Raw editing preset

Plays a Photoshop Action you recorded once (Window > Actions > record a Camera Raw Filter step) before a file uploads - from the Photoshop panel's own Upload button, and from the After Effects panel's "Photoshop + Upload" button. For After Effects frames specifically, the frame is added as a new layer at the bottom of whatever document is currently active in Photoshop (e.g. your particles/overlay template) - the Action runs on that layer, and the composited result uploads. Leave both fields empty to skip this step and upload as-is.

This is a Pro feature - upgrade your tier to use it.
